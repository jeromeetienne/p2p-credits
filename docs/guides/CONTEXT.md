# Directory Context: `/docs/guides`

## Purpose
One document per task, for a reader who already knows the vocabulary and has come here to perform that one task.

## Key Exports & Entry Points
- `price_a_new_task_type.md`: measure a reference cost, and add the task type.
- `choose_a_settlement_policy.md`: immediate credit, provisional credit, or delayed settlement.
- `choose_a_penalty.md`: the five penalties, and what each one costs an honest worker.
- `compare_non_deterministic_results.md`: the four comparison strategies, and when each one holds.
- `set_the_cost_of_an_identity.md`: what stops a Sybil attacker from paying for an identity.

## Rules
- A guide is complete on its own: a reader who opens one guide and reads nothing else can perform the task.
- A guide names the exact parameter and the exact exported name, and links to `../reference/` for the full signature rather than restating it, because a restated signature is the first thing to go stale.
- A guide states what a choice costs as well as what it buys, because every choice offered here trades one of the four families of metrics against another.

## Background
- The split between a lesson, a guide, a reference document, and an explanation is stated in [the context of `/docs`](../CONTEXT.md).
